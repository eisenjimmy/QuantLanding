Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026 · Last updated: July 28, 2026

Quant is designed as a no-account market-analysis app. This policy explains the information handled by the app, the services it contacts, and the choices available to you.

1. Who we are

Quant is an iPhone market-analysis application. For privacy questions, contact the developer at xjimmypark@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Quant stores the following information locally on your device to make the app work:

  • Watchlist, alerts, chart preferences, notification preferences, and display settings.
  • Saved forecast records and selected forecast views.
  • Quant AI conversation content, when you use the AI feature.
  • Optional API credentials, if you enter them in a future supported integration; those credentials are stored using the iOS Keychain.

Quant does not require an account, collect a profile, connect to a brokerage account, or store payment-card information.

3. Market data and news requests

To show market data, charts, symbol search results, and headlines, Quant sends network requests to Yahoo Finance endpoints and requests macroeconomic series from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED service. These requests include the market symbol or data series needed for the request. Like other internet services, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and device/network metadata under their own privacy practices.

4. Advertising

Users without an active Quant Pro subscription may see interstitial ads served through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad personalization according to their own policies and your applicable device or account settings.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

Quant Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Quant does not receive or store your payment-card number. Apple handles transaction records and subscription management under Apple’s privacy practices.

6. Apple Intelligence and Quant AI

On compatible devices, Quant AI uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Quant does not send your Quant AI prompts to a third-party cloud AI provider. Apple Intelligence availability and processing are subject to Apple’s device settings and policies.

7. Notifications and sharing

Notifications are optional and used only for market or signal alerts you enable. Quant uses local notifications and requests permission through iOS. If you choose to share a Quant image or link, iOS sends the content only to the destination you select.

8. How long information is retained

Local preferences and locally saved content remain on your device until you change them or delete the app. Forecast records are managed by the app and expire after seven days. Quant does not operate a user-account database for this information.

9. Your choices and deletion

You can change notification and advertising-related device settings in iOS, manage your Quant Pro subscription through Apple, and remove local Quant data by deleting the app from your device. Because Quant does not maintain user accounts, there is no separate server-side account record for us to delete.

10. Children

Quant is not directed to children. Do not use Quant if you are not old enough to consent to data processing where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Quant changes. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email xjimmypark@gmail.com.
