Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026 · Last updated: July 30, 2026

Quantactic is designed as a no-account market-analysis app. This policy explains the information handled by the app, the services it contacts, and the choices available to you.

1. Who we are

Quantactic is an iPhone market-analysis application. For privacy questions, contact the developer at xjimmypark@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Quantactic stores the following information locally on your device to make the app work:

  • Watchlist, alerts, chart preferences, notification preferences, language preference, and display settings.
  • Saved forecast records and selected forecast views.
  • Quantactic AI conversation content, when you use the AI feature.
  • Optional provider credentials, if you enter them for a supported integration; those credentials are stored using the iOS Keychain.

Quantactic does not require an account, collect a profile, connect to a brokerage account, or store payment-card information.

3. Market data and news requests

To show market data, charts, symbol search results, and headlines, Quantactic sends network requests to market-data providers (currently Yahoo Finance endpoints) and requests macroeconomic series from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED service. These requests include the market symbol or data series needed for the request. Like other internet services, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and device/network metadata under their own privacy practices.

4. Advertising

Users without an active Quantactic Pro subscription may see interstitial ads served through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad personalization according to their own policies and your applicable device or account settings.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

Quantactic Pro subscriptions (monthly and annual) are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Quantactic does not receive or store your payment-card number. Apple handles transaction records and subscription management under Apple’s privacy practices. U.S. reference prices are $4.99 per month and $39.99 per year; regional prices are shown by Apple.

6. Apple Intelligence and Quantactic AI

On compatible devices, Quantactic AI uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. Quantactic does not send your AI prompts to a third-party cloud AI provider. Apple Intelligence availability and processing are subject to Apple’s device settings and policies. Free users have a limited daily AI question allowance; Pro unlocks expanded private AI use.

7. Notifications and sharing

Notifications are optional and used only for market or signal alerts you enable. Quantactic uses local notifications and requests permission through iOS. If you choose to share a Quantactic image or link, iOS sends the content only to the destination you select.

8. How long information is retained

Local preferences and locally saved content remain on your device until you change them or delete the app. Forecast records remain on device for evaluation and product history for an extended period after generation, then expire. Quantactic does not operate a user-account database for this information.

9. Your choices and deletion

You can change notification and advertising-related device settings in iOS, manage your Quantactic Pro subscription through Apple, and remove local Quantactic data by deleting the app from your device. Because Quantactic does not maintain user accounts, there is no separate server-side account record for us to delete.

10. Children

Quantactic is not directed to children. Do not use Quantactic if you are not old enough to consent to data processing where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Quantactic changes. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email xjimmypark@gmail.com.
